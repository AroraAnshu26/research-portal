different directions. SUPPORTING the assurance seam in the one way it has never
been supported, with a price: $300.0 million changed hands inside a testing

test market is a standing counterexample where testing is bought at scale. THE
BOUND, and it is the whole item: what Teradyne bought is production test of
silicon by a test-equipment vendor taking a supply-chain stake, not a third
party attesting to a learned policy, and the property under test there is
physically measurable by an instrument. Nothing read says Teradyne bought
anything resembling policy evaluation. SUPPORTING and WEAKENING in the same
paper, which is why it is one item: HATCH states the thesis's own claim as its
first sentence, that capability claims in manipulation are unverifiable and
existing leaderboards are gameable, and then releases the specifications, build
guide, bill of materials and scoring rubrics under an open licence with no
buyer. That is the commons argument arriving for an eighth time on this exact

twin-certification: an independent group with no stake measured NVIDIA's own
