## THE ONE THING

The federal regime that decides who may accredit removed the requirement that
the reviewers be peers. The Department of Education's final rule of 30 October
redefines an accrediting agency as one conducting activities "through
voluntary, non-Federal review, that may include peer review". The Department's
own line: "By removing the requirement for peer review, agencies have
flexibility to innovate and create a unique review process for institutions and
programs." Its reason is jurisdictional, not evidentiary: the requirement "is
not prescribed in statute under the HEA". These agencies gate Federal student
aid programs "which currently provide more than $100 billion in Pell Grants and
Federal student loans annually". Effective 2027-07-01.
[FR](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/30/2026-22174/accreditation-innovation-and-modernization-the-secretarys-recognition-of-accrediting-agencies)
