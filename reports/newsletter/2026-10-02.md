## FROM THE STOCK SIDE

Serialising `reports/terrain-map.md` §1, the constraint.

Accredited competence, a people constraint rather than a capital one. The binding text is Article 30(8): "The remuneration of the top-level management and the personnel responsible for carrying out the conformity assessment tasks shall not depend on the number of conformity assessments carried out or on the results of those assessments." That single sentence removes the two levers a normal services business would use to scale, paying assessors on volume and paying them on outcome. Growth therefore comes only from hiring and accrediting more assessors, which is slow. Per the register, that headcount sits in few institutions: 7 of the 41 bodies are DGUV Test units of one German body ([CELEX 32023R1230](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32023R1230)).
