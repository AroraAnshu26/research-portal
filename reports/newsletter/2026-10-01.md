## THE ONE THING

The EU corrected the sentence deciding whether an AI system is a safety component. A corrigendum to Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, published 29 September, replaces one word in new AI Act Article 6(1b)'s German text: "Unbeschadet des Absatzes 1a" becomes "Ungeachtet des Absatzes 1a". The English reads "Notwithstanding paragraph 1a, AI systems the failure or malfunctioning of which would endanger health and safety shall qualify as safety components." The published German used "Unbeschadet" there and eleven times elsewhere, and "Ungeachtet" nowhere, so for 67 days the clause overriding the carve-out was worded like one preserving it. ([corrigendum](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1d32c7c2-bb9e-11f1-81de-01aa75ed71a1.0001), [English text](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32026R1744)) · *geo · primary-filing · two-source*

`buried`: Art. 6(1a), new to the board, excludes AI systems "solely used for non-safety related aspects of user assistance, performance optimisation, service efficiency, automation or convenience or quality control".
