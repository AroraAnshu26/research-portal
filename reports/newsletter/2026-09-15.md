## THE ONE THING

Hiroki Sawada and Shunichi Kasahara published a benchmark on 14 September showing that aggregate rollout success rates cannot detect per-instance regression in a robot policy. LIBERO-CTRL pairs each initial state across six single-axis perturbation conditions and a matched simultaneous condition. The paper states that "even when the difference between the two transition rates is not statistically distinguishable from zero, as many as 29.0% of matched initial states still change outcome", reaching 34.5% in the most affected condition, across six policies and three severity levels. Its conclusion: "matched per-instance evaluation is needed to reveal how joint perturbations alter behavior." ([arXiv 2609.15940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15940))

## FROM THE STOCK SIDE

Serialising `reports/terrain-map.md` section 1, industrial certification and standards bodies as a business. The vocabulary, first half.

Conformity assessment is the act of demonstrating that a product meets a legal requirement. Self-certification is the manufacturer doing it alone and signing a declaration. A notified body is a private company that a Member State has authorised to perform third-party conformity assessment, and the word "notified" is literal: the state notifies the Commission of it, and the register is called NANDO. A national accreditation body is a separate organisation that attests the candidate meets the competence requirements, which is a different step from being notified.
