## THE ONE THING

The EPA published a final rule on 17 September, effective 16 November 2026, repealing "the CCS-based standards for new base load stationary combustion turbines (i.e., Phase 2 standards)" and most of the 2024 Carbon Pollution Standards. It states its own driver: "Increasing amounts of large commercial and industrial loads, particularly those related to data center demand for artificial intelligence (AI) applications, are primarily driving this nearly 2.6-fold increase in projected growth rates." New base load turbines built for that load carry no carbon capture standard. ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/17/2026-19071/partial-repeal-of-the-carbon-pollution-standards-for-fossil-fuel-fired-electric-generating-units)) · *geo · primary-filing · single-source*

`buried`: none. The rule removes an obligation and creates no testing, certification, attestation or third-party verification requirement.

## FROM THE STOCK SIDE

Serialising `reports/terrain-map.md` section 1, the vocabulary, second half, which completes it.

A **module** is a named assessment route: module A is internal production control, module B is EU type-examination, module G is unit verification, module H is full quality assurance. A **harmonised standard** is a technical standard cited in the Official Journal whose application gives a **presumption of conformity**, meaning that following it is legally treated as meeting the requirement. **CE marking** is the manufacturer's claim that the whole process was followed. **Annex I** of the Machinery Regulation is the list of product categories that lose the self-certification option.
