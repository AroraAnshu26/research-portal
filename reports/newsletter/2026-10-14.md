## THE ONE THING

The EU cited its first harmonised standard under the AI Act. Commission
Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274, published 14 October and in force that
day, publishes the reference of EN 18286:2026, "Artificial intelligence –
Quality management system for EU AI Act regulatory purposes". It is the only entry in
the Annex and answers point 9 of the 2025 standardisation request, on "quality
management systems for providers of AI systems, including post-market
monitoring processes". No earlier citation exists in 5,000 sector-3 documents
since the AI Act was published. Machinery Article 20(10) carries it
across: AI Act harmonised standards confer presumption of conformity under the
Machinery Regulation until machinery ones exist. The register still returns
zero notified bodies under the AI Act.
