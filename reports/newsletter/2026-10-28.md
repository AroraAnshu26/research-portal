## THE ONE THING

A federal agency deleted the only mandatory transparency test for clinical AI
and told users to ask the vendor instead. ONC's final rule removes the source
attribute, source attribute access and intervention risk management
requirements at 45 CFR 170.315(b)(11), the "model cards" for predictive
decision support. "A large majority of commenters were not supportive of our
proposal." ONC's reason: "we have identified no evidence through peer reviewed
publication or trade press indicating that users of certified health IT have
relied on the transparency requirements". Its answer: "We encourage users of
certified health IT to ask their developers for such information." Present
value of cost savings, $1.55 billion. Effective 28 December 2026.

**Somebody ran the counterfactual the surviving thesis says cannot be run, and
the standard estimate overstates it.** arXiv 2610.37196, 27 October, introduces
"a denoised counterfactual protocol, based on bitwise-reproducible branching,
repeated continuations, held-out oracles, and common random numbers". Across
four world action models and over 100,000 branched rollouts, "bounds from 17
continuations per candidate place its selectable share between 24% and 66%".

**An external monitor repaired a policy it was not allowed to open.** arXiv
2610.36852, 27 October, asks: "When the policy is frozen or inaccessible, can
an external monitor diagnose and repair corrupted inputs without access to its
internals?" On simulated Go2, offline diagnosis raises walking success from
259/640 to 429/640 episodes, online repair reaches 382/640, "with no triggers
in 64 held-out fault-free episodes". Authors' limit: "limited to one policy and
a partly screened simulated fault suite".
