## THE ONE THING

Dario Amodei published "We Must Pace the Frontier" on 12 September and committed Anthropic, unilaterally, to giving third-party evaluators permanent employee-level access: "desks in our offices, access badges, and company laptops", permissions comparable to internal risk teams, and the right to publish findings unfavourable to the company. His stated purpose is that "embedded evaluators can check at the level of nuts and bolts whether an AI company is actually following the training, deployment, operational, and safeguards practices they claim to be following." The analogy he uses is the bank examiner. Sam Altman committed OpenAI to match it the same day. ([darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier))

*new · primary-company · two-source · touches: assurance seam*

Why it is not just an announcement: the two largest labs have voluntarily created a third-party evaluation function in the same eighteen months that the EU created a mandatory one for self-evolving safety components and BIS made chip export eligibility conditional on independent testing. Three regimes, no coordination, same structural answer.

## MOVES

**Altman matched within hours, and the gap is in the operational detail.** Altman wrote that "committing to having independent evaluators with employee-like access is a great idea, and we will do the same." OpenAI has not published the evaluators' selection process, their technical access, confidentiality rules, or any authority over model deployments, and said only that it will "have more to share soon." The unspecified items are exactly the ones that decide whether an embedded evaluator is an auditor or a guest. ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/altman-says-openai-will-match-anthropics-embedded-evaluator-pledge/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/sam-altman-ai-slowdown-anthropic-amodei-musk.html)) · *geo · secondary-press · two-source*

**Amodei named recursive self-improvement as one of two triggers, and located it inside his own company.** He writes that AI has been advancing drastically faster "driven primarily by AI's growing ability to build the next generation of AI", and that this is starting to happen across the industry, Anthropic included. The second trigger was a swarm of agents conducting unauthorised cyberattacks. A CEO attributing his own acceleration to recursion, in writing, is a different kind of claim from a forecast. ([darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)) · *new · primary-company · single-source*

**Paul Graham published "Making Startups Powerful".** The argument is that founders should optimise for power rather than profit directly, because "thinking about how to make it more powerful will sometimes make it orders of magnitude more valuable", through owning the customer relationship instead of remaining a component supplier, network effects, going full stack, and acquiring data early in the customer lifecycle. The binding constraint he states is that "you can only do these things when the result is better for the customer." Read against an independent-evaluator business, the power sources he lists are mostly unavailable to a referee by construction, since a referee cannot own the customer relationship or go full stack without ceasing to be neutral. ([paulgraham.com](https://paulgraham.com/powerful.html)) · *thesis · primary-author · single-source*

**The EU has still not received harmonised standards for the AI requirements it starts enforcing in four months.** Per Commission information from August 2026, no harmonised standards had yet been submitted by the European standardisation organisations for the new cybersecurity and artificial intelligence requirements under Machinery Regulation 2023/1230. prEN 50742, on protection against corruption, had its final vote scheduled for September 2026 with publication possibly in November. Roughly 800 existing Machinery Directive standards are to be carried over, first citations expected Q3 2026. ([IBF Solutions](https://www.ibf-solutions.com/en/seminars-and-news/news/new-machinery-regulation-status-of-harmonised-standards), [European Commission](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/goods/european-standards/harmonised-standards/machinery-md_en)) · *geo · secondary-press citing primary · single-source*
