## THE ONE THING

A federal regulator published how little of the evidence it reads.
FDA's ASCA collection, renewed 23 October under OMB control number 0910-0889:
"FDA generally accepts determinations by ASCA-accredited testing laboratories
that a device conforms with an eligible standard unless FDA determines that a
particular conformity determination should not be accepted", and "does not
intend to review complete test reports from ASCA-accredited testing
laboratories ... except in certain circumstances". Labs accredit against
ISO/IEC 17025:2017. FDA sizes the scheme at 8 accreditation bodies, 150
testing laboratories and 880 test reports a year, and cuts expected new
accreditation applications "from 150 to 50 respondents".
([FR](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/23/2026-21712/agency-information-collection-activities-submission-for-office-of-management-and-budget-review))
