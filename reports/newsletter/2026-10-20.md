## MOVES

**The counterfactual got built out of the log instead of the simulator.**
Cartender, arXiv 2610.26390, 19 October: "Counterfactual Branching constructs
matched and mismatched instruction-action pairs from real recordings without a
simulator". Instruction following "is evaluated by swapping instructions under
identical recorded inputs and measuring behavior rather than agreement with
generated targets". Paired instruction success goes "from 23.67% to 57.00%"; the
gate cuts "infeasible-instruction execution from 53.36% to 14.18%". The branches
are proposed by a model, not observed.
([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26390))
· *thesis · paper · single-source*

**Somebody tested the things that judge the robot.** RoboValue, arXiv
2610.26663, 19 October, holds that "existing evaluations cover only selected
aspects of execution understanding, leaving the reliability of value feedback
insufficiently tested", across 15 simulation and 20 real-world tasks. It finds
"errors in fine-grained instruction grounding, history-dependent progress
tracking, and recovery assessment, even in models with strong outcome and
forward-progress scores".
([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26663))
· *thesis · paper · single-source*
