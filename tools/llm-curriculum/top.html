A

The story in one pass

Modern LLMs are not one invention. They are four layers stacked over twenty years: a sequence model trained to predict, a post-training stage that turns prediction into helpful behaviour, inference-time computation that lets the model think and check, and a harness that connects it to tools, files and environments. Robotics is now repeating the same four layers with actions in place of words.
